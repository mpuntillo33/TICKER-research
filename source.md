## Citation rule for the report

Use the source immediately after the claim it supports. For example: “ACI reduced FY2026 adjusted-EBITDA guidance to $3.550bn-$3.625bn. [Company earnings release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1646972/000164697226000041/aciq126earningsrelease.htm)” Do not cite a call replay without a speaker and timestamp.
