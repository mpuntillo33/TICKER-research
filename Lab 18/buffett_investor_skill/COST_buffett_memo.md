# Costco (COST) Buffett quality memo

## Own the business

Costco shows a durable five-year record in the EDGAR report. Revenue rose from 226,954 to 303,154, net income rose from 5,844 to 9,226, and owner earnings rose from 3,501 to 9,382. The annual ROE values were 28.3%, 25.1%, 31.2%, 27.8%, and 25.8%.

The ROE check passed, owner earnings were positive every year, and owner earnings grew from the first year to the last. The one-year dip in owner earnings to 6,629 does not erase the longer record of cash generation. On this limited quality evidence, I would own the business.

This is a judgment of business quality, not a price judgment or investment recommendation.

Decision reflection: I trust this verdict because every fact used above was printed from the SEC EDGAR 10-K computation and every required check passed.
