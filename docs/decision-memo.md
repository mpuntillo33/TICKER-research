## Decision and committee ask

**Watch/defer. Do not commit capital now.** Valuation range **$13–$19 per share** (base **$17.89**), against a
price of $13.09. The base case sits above the price only because it assumes ACI's cost ratio improves. The market
is pricing no improvement, and ACI has just withdrawn guidance. **Ask:** approve watch/defer, place ACI on the
monitoring list, and revisit at Q3 FY2026 results and the Q4 FY2026 turnaround plan.

## Evidence and as-of date

- **Operations are weakening.** Identical sales excluding fuel fell 0.8% in both Q1 and Q2 FY2026; Q2 adjusted
  EBITDA was $784.0m (4.1% of sales, down from 4.5%) and net income $51.5m. On Oct 13, 2026 ACI withdrew FY2026
  guidance pending a comprehensive business review, with a turnaround plan due by the Q4 FY2026 call. (Q2 FY2026
  earnings release, Form 8-K, Oct 13, 2026.)
- **The value depends on cost discipline.** A five-year three-statement pro-forma drives an FCFF DCF (WACC 7.7%,
  terminal growth 2.0%). Long-run SG&A as a share of gross profit is the dominant driver: each 0.5 percentage point
  moves value about $2.60 per share. Base assumes 94.0% in FY2026 falling to 93.0% by FY2030.
- **The market is pricing no recovery.** A reverse DCF at $13.09 implies a FY2030 SG&A/gross-profit ratio of
  **93.9%**, essentially this year's 94.0%. Holding 94.0% flat gives **$12.69**, below the price.
- **Bridge (Sept 12, 2026):** EV $18,553m − debt incl. finance leases $9,328.2m − opioid settlement obligation
  $773.8m (10-K Note 12) + cash $282.0m = equity $8,733m ÷ 488.2m diluted shares = **$17.89**.

Kroger merger price ($34.10, blocked Dec 2024) is excluded as stale control-and-synergy evidence.
