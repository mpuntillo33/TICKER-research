**Decision user:** The fund's investment committee  
**Company:** Albertsons Companies, Inc. (NYSE: ACI; CIK 0001646972)  
**Valuation date:** February 28, 2026 (the fiscal year-end balance-sheet date)  
**Filing used:** Form 10-K, accession 0001646972-26-000032, filed April 27, 2026

## Initial decision and thesis

**Initial view: Watch / defer.** Albertsons generated $83.173 billion of FY2025 revenue and operates at meaningful scale, but the available starting record shows $8.748 billion of net debt. Before initiating, the committee needs a sustainable earnings and free-cash-flow view, a normalized debt reduction path, and a valuation that produces an adequate equity return after the enterprise-to-equity bridge.

This is deliberately a research posture, not a recommendation to buy or sell. It is based on one annual filing and no market-price, earnings-quality, peer, or forward-estimate work yet.

## Enterprise-to-equity bridge check

Using the filing's balance-sheet inputs:

```text
Equity value = Enterprise value - total debt + cash
             = Enterprise value - $8,946.6m + $198.6m
             = Enterprise value - $8,748.0m
```

At 547.2 million diluted weighted-average shares, net debt represents $15.99 per diluted share. This is a mechanical bridge check only; the filing does not provide the enterprise-value assumption.

## Consequential assumptions

- The valuation date uses the 10-K balance-sheet date, February 28, 2026, so cash and debt are aligned to that date.
- Total debt is defined as the filing's “long-term debt and capital lease obligations including current maturities.” This includes capital lease obligations; any alternative lease treatment must be applied consistently to both enterprise value and operating metrics.
- Cash is fully available to offset debt for this preliminary bridge. Restricted-cash treatment requires confirmation before a final valuation.
- Diluted weighted-average shares are a proxy for equity-value-per-share conversion. A final recommendation should use a current fully diluted share count and incorporate all relevant dilutive securities.

## Material unknowns

- Current share price, market capitalization, and a valuation-date enterprise value.
- Normalized EBITDA, EBIT, free cash flow, working-capital needs, and capital expenditures.
- Debt maturities, interest-rate exposure, covenants, refinancing capacity, and lease-adjusted leverage.
- Comparable-company valuation, precedent transactions, and an intrinsic-value range.
- Same-store-sales trends, gross-margin drivers, competitive position, and the durability of revenue and cash flow.
- Capital-allocation priorities, dividends or repurchases, litigation/regulatory exposure, and management incentives.

## Research plan

1. Obtain the valuation-date share price and calculate market capitalization and enterprise value using the same cash/debt convention above.
2. Build FY2023–FY2025 income statement, cash-flow, and leverage history from the 10-K; reconcile GAAP to any non-GAAP measures.
3. Normalize EBITDA and free cash flow for nonrecurring items, leases, working capital, and maintenance capital expenditures.
4. Analyze debt maturities, interest expense, liquidity, and downside covenant/refinancing risk.
5. Test operating durability through same-store sales, margin, store-base productivity, customer mix, competitive positioning, and key risks disclosed in the filing.
6. Value the company using DCF and comparable-company approaches, then bridge each enterprise-value case to equity value and per-share value using fully diluted shares.
7. Define upside, base, and downside cases; identify catalysts, disconfirming evidence, and a clear decision threshold for initiation.
