## Memo thesis (keep)

ACI's value depends almost entirely on whether the turnaround restores cost discipline.
Long-run SG&A / gross profit is the dominant driver: across its historical band (90.4%–96.0%)
value per share swings from about $3 to about $30. Revenue growth and gross margin matter far less
over their defensible ranges. The market price ($13.09, Oct 16, 2026) implies a long-run SG&A /
gross-profit ratio of about 94.2% (reverse DCF) — no cost recovery at all from the FY2026 base of
94.0%. So the question for the committee is not "is ACI cheap?" but "will the turnaround plan
(due by the Q4 FY2026 call) produce evidence that SG&A / gross profit is falling?" Until it does,
the evidence supports watch/defer, with an initiation trigger tied to that ratio.
